The Challenge

The blockchain ecosystem has matured rapidly, but each project implements its own unique, often inconsistent deployment practices.

Current Issues

  • • Inconsistent documentation
  • • Unstandardized configurations
  • • Divergent packaging methods
  • • Manual setup requirements

Impact

  • • Increased operational costs
  • • Slower adoption rates
  • • Scalability challenges
  • • Operational risks

Our Solution

Define and promote open, reproducible, and automation-friendly standards for blockchain deployments.
